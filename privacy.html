Privacy policy (draft)

This is a simple, practical policy for the MVP website. Customize it for your real data storage and compliance needs before going live.

What we collect

  • Contact info (name, phone, email)
  • Booking details (service type, city/area, date/time, duration)
  • Optional notes you provide (e.g., pickup location, hospital name)

Why we collect it

  • To confirm scope, availability, and pricing
  • To coordinate with helpers and provide updates
  • To improve service quality

How the MVP site works

The booking form generates a structured WhatsApp message (and email fallback). Depending on your device/browser, this may open WhatsApp/email apps and transmit the message through those providers.

Local draft saving

To prevent losing your progress, the booking form may save a draft in your browser (localStorage). You can clear it anytime from the booking page.

Drafts are stored on your device, not on our servers (in the MVP).

Sharing and retention

  • We share only what’s needed to deliver the service (e.g., helper assignment details).
  • Retention periods depend on your operational setup. Specify exact timeframes here before launch.
  • You can request deletion of your data via the contact details below.
